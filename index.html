Our Heritage

Rooted in the
Soil of Kutch

Nestled in Khombhdi Moti, the Chauhan family orchard has been producing premium Kutchi Kesar mangoes for generations. The unique laterite soil of Kutch, combined with the warm coastal climate, gives our Kesar mangoes their signature saffron-golden hue, honey-like sweetness, and royal aroma.

Every mango that leaves our farm is grown with patience and care. No artificial ripening agents. No shortcuts. Just nature, time, and generations of farming wisdom.

  • All orders and reservations are subject to seasonal availability.

  • Quantities are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

  • Prices are set seasonally and may vary based on harvest conditions, grade, and quantity.
  • An advance is required to confirm all reservations; balance is payable before dispatch.
  • All transactions are in Indian Rupees (INR).

  • Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by courier, weather, or agricultural conditions.
  • We are not liable for delays caused by third-party courier partners after dispatch.

🔄 Returns & Quality

  • Due to the perishable nature of fresh produce, returns are not accepted once dispatched.
  • If goods arrive significantly damaged, contact us within 24 hours with photos for case-by-case review.
  • Minor variation in size, weight, and colour is natural for farm produce and does not constitute a defect.

🌿 Product Assurance

  • Our mangoes are grown using traditional orchard practices without harmful chemical ripening agents.
  • Harvest quantities may vary due to natural agricultural and weather conditions.
  • Where primary tree yield falls short, reserve produce is used to meet guaranteed quantities.
