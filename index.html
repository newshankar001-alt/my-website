Our Orchard Story

Rooted in the
Soil of Kutch

At BookAMango, we do not just grow mangoes — we grow trust, tradition, and farm-to-home freshness. Our Kutch orchard is carefully nurtured to bring premium Kutchi Kesar mangoes directly from our trees to your family.

The unique soil of Kutch, combined with the warm Gujarat climate, gives our Kesar mangoes their signature saffron-golden hue, honey-like sweetness, and royal aroma. Every mango that leaves our farm is grown with patience and care — no artificial ripening agents, no shortcuts.

Terms & Conditions

By placing an order or reservation with Chauhan Mango Farm / BookAMango.in, you agree to the following terms.

📦 Orders & Availability

  • All orders and reservations are subject to seasonal availability.
  • Booking confirmation is issued only after advance payment is received.
  • Seasonal reservation packages do not constitute ownership of agricultural land or trees.
  • Quantities are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

💰 Pricing & Payment

  • Prices are set seasonally and may vary based on harvest conditions, grade, and quantity.
  • An advance is required to confirm all reservations; balance is payable before dispatch.
  • All transactions are in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • Delivery charges are extra and calculated based on location and order quantity.

🚚 Delivery

  • Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by courier, weather, or agricultural conditions.
  • We are not liable for delays caused by third-party courier partners after dispatch.
  • Customers must ensure someone is available to receive perishable goods on delivery.
  • Final delivery cost is calculated at checkout based on pincode and order quantity.

🔄 Returns & Quality

  • Due to the perishable nature of fresh produce, returns are not accepted once dispatched.
  • If goods arrive significantly damaged, contact us within 24 hours with photos for case-by-case review.
  • Minor variation in size, weight, and colour is natural for farm produce and does not constitute a defect.

🌿 Product Assurance

  • Our mangoes are grown using traditional orchard practices without harmful chemical ripening agents.
  • Harvest quantities may vary due to natural agricultural and weather conditions.
  • Where primary tree yield falls short, reserve produce is used to meet guaranteed quantities.

📋 General

  • These terms may be updated without prior notice. Current terms apply at time of order.
  • Disputes shall be subject to jurisdiction in Kutch, Gujarat, India.
  • For queries, contact us at +91 74484 25888 via WhatsApp or call.
