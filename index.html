About Us

BookAMango.in was created to reconnect families with authentic farm-fresh Kutchi Kesar mango experiences directly from the orchards of Gujarat.


Operating from the agricultural heartland of Kutch, Gujarat, we combine traditional farming strengths with modern processing infrastructure to deliver products that meet premium quality expectations.


Kesar mango, also celebrated as the “Queen of Mangoes,” is known for its rich aroma, vibrant saffron-colored pulp, and luxurious sweetness.
